============================================================
RECNEXUS CUSTOM DOMAIN EMAIL CONFIGURATION GUIDE
============================================================

1. Domain Server & MX Setup:
   - Domain: recnexus.com
   - Support Email: support@recnexus.com
   - Staff Email Formats:
     * Firstname.Lastname@recnexus.com (e.g., Abbie.Potter@recnexus.com)
     * Gamertag@recnexus.com (e.g., Coach.RecNexus@recnexus.com)

2. Recommended Free/Managed Business Email Providers:
   - Zoho Mail (Free tier allows custom domain hosting like support@recnexus.com)
   - Cloudflare Email Routing (Free redirection to your personal inbox)
   - Google Workspace / Microsoft 365 (Paid enterprise options)

3. DNS Records Required on your Domain Registrar:
   - MX Records (to route mail to your provider)
   - SPF Record: v=spf1 include:mx.zoho.com ~all (example)
   - DKIM & DMARC records for secure authentication.
